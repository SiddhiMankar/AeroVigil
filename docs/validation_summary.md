## 3. Key Technical Verification Highlights

### A. Digital Twin Expected-State Independence
The Digital Twin (`src/digital_twin.py`) predicts expected engine states ($\mathbf{x}_{\text{exp}}$) strictly from healthy operating conditions ($\text{throttle}, T_{\text{amb}}, \text{phase}$) without inspecting scenario labels. Observed vs Expected residuals ($\mathbf{z}$) cleanly reveal injected anomalies.

### B. Sensor Drift vs Physical Fault Isolation
* In **`overheating`**, CHT ($+12.3\sigma$), EGT ($+6.5\sigma$), and Oil Temp ($+17.7\sigma$) are all elevated, leading to `OVERHEATING` classification and `CONSIDER_MISSION_ABORT` advisory.
* In **`sensor_drift`**, only CHT ($+14.2\sigma$) is elevated while EGT ($< 0.1\sigma$) and Oil Temp ($< 0.3\sigma$) remain nominal, leading to `SENSOR_DRIFT` classification, `MODERATE` risk ($32.4$), and `INSPECT_AT_NEXT_OPPORTUNITY` advisory.
