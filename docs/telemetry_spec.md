# AeroVigil DT — Telemetry Specification & Physics Model

## 1. Overview

This document specifies the synthetic aero-piston engine telemetry model developed for **Phase 1 of AeroVigil DT**.

> **DISCLAIMER**: The dataset values, numerical ranges, and dynamics described herein are **synthetic prototype values** created to demonstrate technical feasibility for SIH 2026. They do **not** represent certified physical aircraft measurements or specific proprietary engine specifications.

---

## 3. Mission Phase Profile

The 20-minute standard flight profile is divided into 8 realistic mission phases:

1. **STARTUP** (0% - 5%): Engine crank, idle (~1200 RPM), alternator activation (~13.8V).
2. **TAKEOFF** (5% - 10%): Full throttle (1.00), maximum RPM (~5600), peak fuel flow (~42 L/h).
3. **CLIMB** (10% - 25%): High power (0.85 throttle, ~5200 RPM), altitude temp drop.
4. **CRUISE** (25% - 55%): Steady throttle (0.65, ~4600 RPM), thermal equilibrium.
5. **MANEUVER** (55% - 70%): Dynamic throttle variations (0.75 avg), RPM transients.
6. **CRUISE** (70% - 85%): Second cruise leg (0.65 throttle).
7. **DESCENT** (85% - 95%): Throttle reduction (0.30, ~3000 RPM), airflow cooling.
8. **LANDING** (95% - 100%): Idle power (0.15 throttle, ~1500 RPM), touchdown.

## 4. Fault Injection Scenarios

Controlled fault injections begin progressively at 30% into the mission profile ($p(t) \in [0, 1]$):

1. **`normal`**: Baseline healthy mission telemetry.
2. **`overheating`**: Simulated cooling air duct blockage / lean fuel mixture.
   - $\Delta CHT = +65.0 \cdot p^{1.2}$ °C
   - $\Delta EGT = +85.0 \cdot p^{1.1}$ °C
   - $\Delta OilTemp = +28.0 \cdot p^{1.2}$ °C
3. **`lubrication_fault`**: Simulated oil pump degradation / oil leak.
   - $\Delta OilPress = -34.0 \cdot p^{1.1}$ psi (drops toward ~15 psi)
   - $\Delta OilTemp = +36.0 \cdot p^{1.2}$ °C (friction heating)
4. **`vibration_anomaly`**: Mechanical unbalance / cylinder misfire.
   - $\Delta Vibration = +0.70 \cdot p^{1.3}$ g (rises above 0.9g)
   - RPM jitter variance $\sigma_{rpm} = 60 \cdot p^{1.5}$ rev/min
5. **`sensor_drift`**: Sensor electrical bias strictly on a single sensor (e.g. CHT).
   - $CHT_{observed} = CHT_{true} + 65.0 \cdot p$ °C
   - **Critical Property**: Correlated physical signals ($EGT$, $OilTemp$, $RPM$, $OilPress$) remain **completely normal**!
